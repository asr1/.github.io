Night Watch (talk about reading it during 2020)
> I'm reluctant to re-read that one. I first read it in 2020, while living in Seattle during the George Floyd protests. Part of downtown was taken over. Police were driven out, and an autonomous zone was declared. Reading that book at that cultural moment was such a powerful, unique experience. I loved it, and I'm afraid the second ring will not echo as loud.
