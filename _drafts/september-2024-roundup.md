Below are things I've learned or found interesting in the past month: 

1. This 2017 blog post, [Reality has a Surprising Amount of Detail.](http://johnsalvatier.org/blog/2017/reality-has-a-surprising-amount-of-detail)

2. Per [this map](https://x.com/amazingmap/status/1835322932282974252/photo/1) by Mark Lotto, only 13 states are entirely north of Mexico and entirely south of Canada. ![US map showing 13 states entirely between US and Canada](statesBetween.png)

3. More in the series of "things you didn't realize were named after people: [Macadamia nuts were named by a botanist after John Macadam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macadamia#Etymology). Wonder if he knew the botanist who [named Fuschia after Leonhart Fuchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuchsia#History). Similarly, shrapnel was [named after Lieutenant-General Henry Shrapnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrapnel_shell). THe more you know!

4. Top-Level domains are the things that appear at the end of URLs, like .com and .gov. Many of these are reserved for countries (.us, .uk and so on). Only one of these, .su, is [not governed by a country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.su). It was assigned to the Soviet Union before its collapse, and the relevant governing bodies have attempted to eliminate it ever since. Currently, Russia governs it, and the domain is largely associated with cybercrime.
