Below are things I've learned or found interesting in the past month: *Late because I forgot the month rolled over!*


1. The Super Mario 64 [strategy guide](https://archive.org/details/super-mario-64-complete-guide-book_202309/page/n95/mode/2up) in Japan had cute dioramas of each of the levels.

2. Courtesy of Experimental History, [running with Ethiopians](https://ccli.substack.com/p/what-ethiopian-runners-taught-me).

3. [Doing bad, drawing anyway](https://substack.com/home/post/p-173908843).

4. Courtesy of Austin Kleon, George Saunders [on reading problematic authors](https://georgesaunders.substack.com/p/a-tough-question-indeed).

5. The Nintendo DS had so many cool applications outside of gaming. [The Louvre had a 3D map and narration app](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-louvre-is-saying-goodbye-to-its-nintendo-3ds-tour-guides/?), which was discontinued earlier in September of this year. And the [Mariners (baseball) used to have an app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Fan_Network) that let you order food or watch replays from your seat. And this was 2007! That's when the first iPhone came out. 

6. Humans can tell which birds are pretty by sound. Or rather, we have [the same preferences as other animals](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.26.661759v2). Neat!
