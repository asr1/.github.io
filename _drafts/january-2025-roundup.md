Below are things I've learned, remembered, or found interesting in the past month: 

1. Courtesy of [Astral Codex 10](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/links-for-december-2024), the [Southern Television Broadcast Interruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Television_broadcast_interruption), where a broadcast was interrupted by a message claiming to be from aliens instructing humanity to put down their weapons and achieve a new ae of peace.

2. [10 things I learned](https://garden.alexrinehart.net/reading-and-writing/books/101-things-i-learned-in-architecture-school/) from *101 Things I Learned in Architecture School* ([affiliate link](https://amzn.to/4fyHbgZ)).

3. Every few years I remember the [story of Shane Perez sneaking](https://shaneperez.blogspot.com/2009/12/discovery-island.html) onto Disney's abandoned Discovery Park. When looking for it to link here, I found [this Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/HobbyDrama/comments/1244wns/urbex_journey_to_discovery_island_the_area_51_of/) detailing all of the explorers who have come since. Lots of copycats, until it finally got shut down.

4. [CoolioArt on Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbCQxBTcyRk&feature=youtu.be) remade the kitchen scene from Jurassic Park with scientifically accurate (feathered) dinos!

5. [The quest](https://lostpixellore.com/blog/where-in-the-world-is-static-shock-for-gba/) to find an unreleased Static Shock GBA game.

6. The [Liberty Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Bell) is overrated. It cracked the first time it was rung, but that wasn't *that* crack. It *may* have rung when the Declaration of Independence was read out, but only because every available bell was wringing. It just sort of got elevated into a symbol of independence because it had a bible quote about freedom on it.

7. This [collection of public domain artwork](https://public.work/) has been going around, and includes some novel ways of exploring the collection.

8. I knew Fortnite was big, but I didn't realize the degree to which it [utterly dominated](https://bsky.app/profile/mrhands31.bsky.social/post/3lfshjryru22n) playtime. Baldur's Gate 3 represented 3.9% of all playtime in 2023. [Initial source](https://www.matthewball.co/all/stateofvideogaming2025) is worth a read. Overall, the video game market appears to be shrinking, down 13% since 2021. One cause (among many) is adults spending leisure hours each day on TikTok.

9. Correction. I [prevously noted]({% link _posts/2024-06-01-may-roundup.md%}) the renaming of Cleaveland to Cleveland, and suggested that it was due to the name not fitting on a newspaper heading. It seems [that is a myth](https://clevelandmagazine.com/in-the-cle/articles/cle-myths-the-a-in-cleaveland).

10. Agony [used to be slang](https://old.reddit.com/r/etymology/comments/1i3j2fh/agony_use_has_me_at_a_loss_ideas/) in the US for a popular craze or fashion. Seen in an add for "The latest agony".

11. China has been able to [create artificial photosynthesis](http://en.people.cn/n3/2025/0121/c90000-20268473.html) in space. They have ambitious plans, including building a [solar array](https://www.livescience.com/space/space-exploration/china-plans-to-build-enormous-solar-array-in-space-and-it-could-collect-more-energy-in-a-year-than-all-the-oil-on-earth) that could annually generate 'more energy than all the oil on earth' and a lunar space station by 2035. I'll believe 'em when I see 'em.

12. In 1914, archeologists [found a dinosaur](https://phys.org/news/2025-01-paleontologists-species-north-african-predatory.html). It was classified as a Carcharodontosaurus (shark toothed lizard) and forgotten about. The fossil was destroyed in WWII. Recently, photographs of the skeleton have surfaced, indicating that th dinosaur is a new species after all.

13. A very simple suggestion from [Knight at the Opera](https://knightattheopera.blogspot.com/2025/01/defeat-not-death.html) to have TTRPG baddies gain the "defeated" condition at 0HP, rather than death. It's elegant, and I'll be using it.
