Below are things I've learned, remembered, or found interesting in the past month: 

1. Courtesy of [Astral Codex 10](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/links-for-december-2024), the [Southern Television Broadcast Interruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Television_broadcast_interruption), where a broadcast was interrupted by a message claiming to be from aliens instructing humanity to put down their weapons and achieve a new ae of peace.

2. [10 things I learned](https://garden.alexrinehart.net/reading-and-writing/books/101-things-i-learned-in-architecture-school/) from *101 Things I Learned in Architecture School* ([affiliate link](https://amzn.to/4fyHbgZ)).

3. Every few years I remember the [story of Shane Perez sneaking](https://shaneperez.blogspot.com/2009/12/discovery-island.html) onto Disney's abandoned Discovery Park. When looking for it to link here, I found [this Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/HobbyDrama/comments/1244wns/urbex_journey_to_discovery_island_the_area_51_of/) detailing all of the explorers who have come since. Lots of copycats, until it finally got shut down.

4. [CoolioArt on Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbCQxBTcyRk&feature=youtu.be) remade the kitchen scene from Jurassic Park with scientifically accurate (feathered) dinos!

5. [The quest](https://lostpixellore.com/blog/where-in-the-world-is-static-shock-for-gba/) to find an unreleased Static Shock GBA game.

6. The [Liberty Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Bell) is overrated. It cracked the first time it was rung, but that wasn't *that* crack. It *may* have rung when the Declaration of Independence was read out, but only because every available bell was wringing. It just sort of got elevated into a symbol of independence because it had a bible quote about freedom on it.
