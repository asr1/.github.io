Below are things I've learned, remembered, or found interesting in the past month: 

1. The word ["Till"](https://www.merriam-webster.com/grammar/should-you-use-until-or-till-or-til) (meaning up to) is [older than](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/till) the word "Until". Until is actually a [lengthening of until](https://www.etymonline.com/word/until)

2. The capital of Washington State is [Olympia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympia,_Washington), the 23rd (or 24th) largest city. It was briefly Vancouver, before that was overturned because of [improperly filed paperwork](https://web.archive.org/web/20070927174307/http://www.columbian.com/history/Vancouver/vancouvercapitol.cfm). Among other things, the bill establishing the capital neglected to include the phrase "Be it enacted".
