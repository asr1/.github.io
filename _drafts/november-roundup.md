Below are things I've learned, remembered, or found interesting in the past month: 

1\. Etymology: The word "ascorbic" in "ascorbic acid" [comes from](https://www.etymonline.com/word/ascorbic) fending off scurvy. "*1933 (in ascorbic acid), from a- (2) "off, away from" + scorbic, scorbutic "of scurvy," from Medieval Latin scorbuticus "scurvy," which is perhaps of German or Dutch origin. Originally in reference to Vitamin C, which is an anti-scorbutic.*"

2\. [Pipe Four](https://pipefour.com/pa/), a search engine for Penny Arcade.

3\. [Ants outnumber people](https://www.nbcnews.com/science/science-news/ants-how-many-number-earth-planet-population-humans-rcna48534) in every single city in the world. For every person, there are more than 2.5 MILLION ants.

6\. Some elevators have a seismic button, which calls the elevator to the nearest floor, opens the doors, and applies brakes. More interestingly, elevators have a [fandom wiki](https://elevation.fandom.com/wiki/Earthquake_emergency_return_(EER))! I can't believe it isn't called "Liftipedia".
