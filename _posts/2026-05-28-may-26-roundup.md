9. The famous Charlie's Angels pose is largely [a myth](https://boards.straightdope.com/t/the-origin-of-the-charlies-angels-pose/942286). In the original, it was gun / karate / walkie talkie (and only in silhouette!). In the remake it was all karate, no guns. The classic 3 gun pose never happened.
