6. I shared this [piece about storage](https://economichardship.org/2024/08/tired-of-losing/)back in March, but I've been thinking about it again recently. I saw a post recently (that I of course cannot find now) where someone framed getting rid of things as "Free storage that has a cost to retrieve them (repurchasing)". Get rid of your cables and screws, they said. Home Depot will keep all the screws you need for a low yearly fee. I like when a conventional framing is twisted on its head, and I'm always a fan of intentional moves away from consumerism and towards minimalism.
