Below is the list of movies I've seen this year, very roughly sorted by how much I enjoyed them (least favorite at the top, favorite at the bottom). Commentary included for select movies.

* **8½ (1963)**
* **Moneyball**
* **Asteroid City**
* **The Limey** *A few truly inspired scenes (taking out the gangsters, crashing the party), the latter of which was only possible due to the frenetic editing. Overall, I found it distracting, despite the novel way of keeping up the pace of expositional scenes.*
 
* **Goldfinger**
* **Venom 2: Let There Be Carnage** *Not nearly as charming as the first. Lots of callbacks to jokes that were done better the first time around.*

* **The Disruptors**
* **Fantastic Four (1994)**
* **Soul Music**
* **But I'm a Cheerleader**
* **Terminator 2: Judgment Day** (rewatch) *Not nearly as good as I remember, and not nearly as tightly scripted as the first one.*

* **The Goldfinch**
* **Fast Times at Ridgemont High**
* **American Beauty**
* **The 40 Year Old Virgin**
* **All the President’s Men**
* **From Russia with Love**
* **Thank You For Smoking**
* **North By Northwest**
* **Frost/Nixon**
* **Love Actually**
* **Terminator** (rewatch)
* **21 Jump Street (2012)** (rewatch) *Brie Larson can act! Turns out her wooden Captain Marvel was a directorial decision.
 
* **Lock, Stock, and Two Smoking barrels** *Basically a game of Fiasco with a baller soundtrack. Unusual cinematography at times.*
* **Game Night**
* **The Mighty Ducks**
* **Murder on the Orient Express (2017)**
* **The Italian Job**
* **Memento**
* **Haywire** *An instant classic in the spy genre. Simple plot, with enough elements to keep things interesting an suspenseful. Adding another genre to Soderbergh's repertoire. And the score positively rules.*

* **Panic Room**
* **Dungeons and Dragons: Honor Among Thieves** *Really fun adventure movie, in the style of The Mummy. The tiefling design was cowardice, but fun all around.*
