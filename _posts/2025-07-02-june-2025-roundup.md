Below are things I've learned, remembered, or found interesting in the past month: 

1. After 200 years, red-billed choughs were [reintroduced](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clyqg9j71eqo) to Kent, England.

2. There is [much debate](https://www.drumforum.org/threads/the-drum-mystery-in-the-beatles-most-beautiful-song.197473/) among Beatles fans over whether Paul actually played the drums on Dear Prudence. This [Medium article](https://medium.com/1ntune/the-beatles-dear-prudence-mystery-drummer-ringo-paul-or-a-deeper-studio-secret-53899718e2ad) covers it pretty well.

3. Courtesy of [Tom Scott's newsletter](https://www.tomscott.com/newsletter/), this delightful [story about fake birds](https://slate.com/culture/2025/05/birds-movies-charlies-angels-2000-pygmy-nuthatch.html) in Charlie's Angels. And you thought investigative journalism was dead.

4. A fascinating [long read](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2025/may/27/the-curse-of-toumai-ancient-skull-disputed-femur-feud-humanity-origins) about paleoanthropologists and very old bones. Lots of strong personalities here!

5. Mildly related, the world's oldest recorded fingerprint may also be the [oldest portable art](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2025/may/26/like-a-face-discovery-reinforces-idea-neanderthals-created-art-say-experts).

6. A fleet of electric cars in Utrecht, Netherlands will [use Vehicle-to-grid charging](https://www.autoweek.com/news/a64992869/vehicle-to-grid-charging-renault-utrecht-netherlands/) to use solar power and the cars' batteries to supplement the power grid. Very cool!

7. [John Green telling the story of Jimmy Glass (sports).](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTqgth5aZOM)

8. I love alien abduction stories, and the first half of [this blog post](https://smoothbrains.net/posts/2025-06-14-how-to-talk-about-ufos-without-alienating-your-friends.html) by Cube Flipper is fascinating. The second half is about neuroscience and drugs, which may interest you more than it does me.

9. Having trouble getting a screw to find purchase? Got a stripped screw hole? Stick a toothpick or two in the hole and try again!

10. [What happened to the V-chip?](https://www.popularmechanics.com/culture/tv/a19408909/20-years-ago-v-chip/). I'd read a much deeper dive into this!

11. Tips for [anyone making a comic](https://bsky.app/profile/reillybrown.bsky.social/post/3lsefv4ze222q).

12. New dino! [This one's dog-sized](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cglzy4zndp0o). As the owner of two very large dogs (110 lb) who are friends with a very small (8 lb) dog, I realize that "dog-sized" is, at best, a vague measurement.

13. [The wrongest bird in movie history](https://slate.com/culture/2025/05/birds-movies-charlies-angels-2000-pygmy-nuthatch.html).

14. Wraps are a [much more recent invention](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(food)) than I would have expected!
