It's time for the annual movie awards! I haven't watched most of what's up for an Oscar, so instead I've given a handful of awards to the 32 movies I've seen this year, in all the standard categories.

## Best foreign movie

The Count of Monte Cristo (2024).   
*It's a perfect movie!
*
## Best Sequel

Aliens.  
*Tight competition with Magic Mike The Last Dance and For a Few Dollars More*

## Best movie despite the supporting cast seemingly learning to act as the film goes on

Gran Torino

## Best movie based on true events

~~Aliens~~ Apollo 13

## Best PG-rated movie

Apollo 13, just barely over Conclave.

##  Best adaption of a novel from more than 200 years ago that has been adapted many times before but has never looked so damn good. In honor of the Oscar's, awards for what I've been watching

Count of Monte Cristo (2024)

## Best Clint Eastwood

For a Few Dollars More.  

*Surprisingly stacked category!*

![Umbrella scene Conclave](conclave.png)

## Prettiest movie

Conclave

## Best Animated Movie

The Iron Giant
